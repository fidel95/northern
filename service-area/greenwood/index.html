What we see in Greenwood homes.

Greenwood sits directly south of Indianapolis, in Johnson County — inside the area our own crews cover, with no subcontractors between you and the people doing the work.

Aluminium frames from the 1960s and 70s

Aluminium conducts heat straight through the frame, so these are the windows that sweat and frost on the inside no matter how good the glass is. Replacing them is the single change most likely to make a difference you can actually feel standing next to the window in February.

Ranch proportions

Ranch and split-level homes tend to have wide, short openings and long runs of window across the front. Those look best as a matched run rather than as individual units replaced one at a time — and pricing a whole elevation at once is usually cheaper per opening than doing it in stages.

Basement and egress windows

Post-war basements here often have small steel or glass-block openings. Those are among the least pleasant windows to live with and among the most straightforward to replace, and they come up often enough that we keep the sizes on hand.
