What we see in Carmel homes.

Carmel sits directly north of Indianapolis, in Hamilton County — inside the area our own crews cover, with no subcontractors between you and the people doing the work.

Subdivision homes from the boom years

The subdivisions that filled in west of Keystone and north toward 146th Street were built with volume-grade double-hung units. Thirty years on, the failure is usually the same: the seal between the panes lets go, moisture gets in, and the glass fogs in a way no amount of cleaning touches. The frame is often still sound — which is exactly the case where a pocket replacement costs less and takes less out of your wall.

Older homes near Old Town and the Arts District

Closer to Main Street the housing is older and the openings are less standard. Those are the ones worth measuring before quoting: an opening that has moved, a sill with water damage behind the trim, or a header that was never sized for the span all change the answer from pocket to full-frame.

Architectural review committees

Most Carmel subdivisions of that era have an ARC that wants to see a spec before you install. We supply the product sheets, frame colour names and grille pattern drawings that a review committee asks for, so the approval isn't something you have to reverse-engineer from a brochure.
