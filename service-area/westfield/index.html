What we see in Westfield homes.

Westfield sits north of Indianapolis, in Hamilton County — inside the area our own crews cover, with no subcontractors between you and the people doing the work.

New houses, early failures

A window that fails at fifteen years failed early, and it usually failed for a reason worth knowing: an undersized unit, a glass package chosen for a milder climate, or an install that never got flashed properly. We look for the cause during the measure, because replacing like with like just resets the clock.

Older farmhouses and homes near downtown

Away from the new subdivisions there is still a good deal of older housing around the original town centre and on the roads out of it. Those are full-frame conversations more often than not, and worth quoting both ways.

Egress and finished basements

A lot of newer Westfield homes finish their basements after the fact. Turning a basement room into a legal bedroom means an egress-sized opening, which means cutting the foundation and pulling a permit. We file it — and we will tell you honestly when a room is not worth converting.
