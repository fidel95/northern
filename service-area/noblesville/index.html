Replacement windows
and doors in Noblesville

Noblesville is two housing stocks in one city: nineteenth- and early twentieth-century homes around the courthouse square and Old Town, and newer subdivisions out toward the edges. They need almost opposite things from a window company.

What we see in Noblesville homes.

Noblesville sits north-east of Indianapolis, in Hamilton County — inside the area our own crews cover, with no subcontractors between you and the people doing the work.

Historic Old Town openings

Older homes here rarely have a standard opening. Arched heads, tall narrow sashes, and original wood casing that is worth more than the window in it are all common. Those get a full-frame quote and a conversation about what stays: in most cases the interior trim and stool never have to come off.

Original wood that should not be replaced

A lot of what gets torn out in a hurry on a historic house cannot be bought back. We measure to the existing opening and order to it, rather than making the opening fit a stock size — the arched casement in our gallery is one of those.

Newer subdivisions on the edges

Out toward 146th and the reservoir the housing is much younger and the work looks like Fishers': volume-grade double-hungs with failed seals, replaced pocket-style in a day or two.
