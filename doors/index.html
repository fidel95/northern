Common questions

Drafts, openings,
and security.

Anything not covered here, ask during the consultation or call (765) 376-1197.

Will a new patio door stop the draft along the floor?

Usually, yes. Most floor draft at a patio door comes from a failed sill sweep or an out-of-square frame rather than the glass. A full-frame replacement re-levels the sill and installs a new interlocking sweep.

Can you widen an existing opening?

Yes, though it becomes a framing job as well as a door job. Widening a patio opening for a multi-slide unit requires a new header sized by span; we quote the carpentry separately and pull the permit.

How secure are sidelights next to a front door?

All sidelight glass is laminated as standard, which holds together when struck instead of breaking out, and we set multi-point locks on the door slab itself rather than a single deadbolt.

Do entry doors come pre-finished?

Paint-grade and stain-grade doors arrive factory-finished in the color you select at the consultation. Field finishing is available but not recommended in Indiana humidity.
