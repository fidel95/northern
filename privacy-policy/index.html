Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED — AUGUST 2026

DRAFT FOR REVIEW — this text is a starting point that covers the Salesforce and reCAPTCHA disclosure obligations created by the forms on this site. It must be reviewed by the business owner and, ideally, counsel before the site goes live.

1. What we collect

When you submit a consultation request, an instant estimate, or a visualizer configuration, we collect the information you type into the form: your first and last name, email address, phone number, city, and the description of the work you are considering.

The estimate and visualizer tools also write a summary of the product configuration you built into that description field, so that a designer can prepare for the conversation. Those tools run entirely in your browser; nothing is transmitted until you submit the form.

2. Why we collect it

We use it for one purpose: to contact you about the window or door work you asked about, and to prepare an accurate quote for it. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not share it with third-party advertisers.

If you become a customer, the same record is used to schedule your measure and installation visits and to administer your warranty.

3. Where it is stored

Form submissions are transmitted to Salesforce, our customer relationship management provider, and stored in our Salesforce organization. Salesforce processes this data on our behalf under its own security and privacy commitments.

Access inside our company is limited to the designers, schedulers, and service staff who need it to do the work.

4. reCAPTCHA

Our forms are protected by Google reCAPTCHA to prevent automated submissions. When a form loads, reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, including device and application data, and sends it to Google for analysis.

That collection is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, and the information is used to provide, maintain, and improve reCAPTCHA and for general security purposes. It is not used for personalized advertising by Google.

5. Cookies and analytics

The site uses a small number of cookies for basic functionality and, if analytics are enabled, to understand which pages visitors use. Analytics cookies do not carry your name, email, or phone number.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The estimating and visualizing tools will continue to work; only page-level measurement is affected.

6. How long we keep it

Consultation requests that do not become projects are retained for twenty-four months, so a designer can reference the earlier conversation if you come back to the project later.

Customer records are retained for the life of the warranty — the lifetime of the installed product — because warranty administration requires the original order detail.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to send you a copy of what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Call the office or use the contact form and we will confirm the request within ten business days.

You can also unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any newsletter email; that does not affect an active project or warranty record.

8. Children

The site is intended for homeowners and is not directed to children under thirteen. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

If you believe a child has submitted information through this site, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be noted at the top of the page for at least thirty days.

Continuing to use the site after a change takes effect means you accept the revised policy.

Questions about this policy, or a request to access or delete your information: call (765) 376-1197 or use the contact form. See also our Terms of Use.
