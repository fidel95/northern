# Hero

The page-top section on the fog-pines photo: `.np-photo.np-hero` with `data-theme="dark"`, holding an eyebrow, a Cinzel headline, a lede and up to two buttons.

- Headline in `display-xl` (40px on phones), six words or fewer.
- Eyebrow lists services or the page's topic, uppercase, separated by " · ".
- One primary button and at most one outline button.
- The `scrim-photo` overlay keeps `on-pine` text at 4.5:1 or better anywhere on the photo; don't remove it or lighten the photo.

Consumer provides: background-image URL (fog-pines by default, or a dark job photo), eyebrow, headline, lede, actions.
