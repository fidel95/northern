# Client contracts

Nothing here ships. `.assetsignore` keeps this directory out of the Cloudflare
assets bundle, so the contract never appears on the public website.

> **Draft: have an Indiana attorney review this agreement before the first job
> is signed on it.** It was written to follow the Indiana Home Improvement
> Contracts Act (IC 24-5-11), the FTC Cooling-Off Rule (16 CFR 429) and
> Indiana's home solicitation rules, but it is not legal advice. Pay particular
> attention to Section 15 (insurance claims), Section 16 (liens) and the
> warranty terms in Section 12.

## `Northern-Pines-Home-Improvement-Agreement.docx`

The agreement reps fill in with the homeowner. It is 8 pages: 6 pages for the
agreement and signatures, then two copies of the Notice of Cancellation.

### One-time setup (office)

Fill these in once in the master copy, so reps never have to:

- `[STREET ADDRESS]` and `[ZIP]`: the office address. They appear in the header
  and on both cancellation notices.
- `[REGISTRATION NO(S).]`: the county contractor registration numbers.
- `[CUSTOMER CONTACT NAME]`, `[CONTACT PHONE]`, `[CONTACT EMAIL]`: the person
  homeowners call with problems.
- Section 4: the default payment percentages, card-fee sentence, holdback
  amount and late-payment terms.
- Section 5: `[LENDER NAME]` and the number of days for the financing
  contingency.
- Section 7: the unit prices for hidden-damage repairs.
- Section 12: `[WORKMANSHIP WARRANTY TERM]`. Also confirm that the windows
  warranty wording matches what you actually offer.
- Section 17: `[COUNTY]`.

Then save it as the master and give each rep a copy.

### Per job (rep)

Every yellow `[PLACEHOLDER]` gets typed over or deleted. Indiana requires these
items in the contract, **completely filled in, before the homeowner signs**:

- Customer name(s) and project address (Section 1).
- The date the agreement is given to the customer, and the date the offer
  expires (top of page 1).
- A reasonably detailed description of the work, with the specifications if
  you have them (Section 2).
- Approximate start and completion dates (Section 6).
- The contract price (Section 3).
- Printed names and signatures for every customer and for the rep (signature
  page).

On both Notice of Cancellation pages, fill in the **date of transaction** (the
signing date) and the **cancel-by date**. The cancel-by date is the third
business day after signing. Under the FTC rule, Saturdays count as business
days; Sundays and federal holidays don't. For example, if the customer signs
on Thursday, the cancel-by date is Monday (Friday, Saturday, Monday).

Leave both Notice of Cancellation copies with the homeowner, along with a
signed copy of the whole agreement.

### Editing tips

- **Find the next blank:** open Find (Ctrl/Cmd + F) and search for `[`.
- **Remove all highlighting at once** to print a clean copy: Select All, then
  set Text Highlight Color to *No Color*.
- **Sections are Word headings**, so View → Navigation Pane gives you a
  clickable outline. Section numbers are typed in by hand: if you add or remove
  a section, update the cross-references ("see Section 7") too.
- **Line-item table:** put the cursor in the last row and press Tab to add a row.
- **Checkboxes** are ☐ characters. To check one, replace it with ☒ (or just
  mark the printed copy by hand).
- The file opens in Word, Google Docs and Pages.
