How it works

Four steps, start to finish

One project or six, every job runs the same way, with the same people answering your questions.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We walk the outside of your home with you, talk through what needs doing now and what can wait, and answer your questions. No pressure, no obligation.

  2. 2

    Estimate

    You get a free, itemized estimate in writing, with every line explained before you sign anything.

  3. 3

    Construction

    Our own crews do the work on the schedule we agreed, keep you updated as it goes, and clean up behind themselves.

  4. 4

    Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished job with you and put right anything that isn’t. You keep your warranty and care instructions in writing.
