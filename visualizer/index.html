What the visualizer is, and isn't.

01

It matches the catalog

Every style, frame color, grille, and glass package here is one we manufacture, and every one is included in all three window tiers — not charged as an extra. Your choices carry into the estimate tool with you. Nothing shown is aspirational.

02

It's a real 3D scene

All three demo homes are real, lit 3D models you can rotate and zoom — not illustrations. Pick the massing closest to yours: a single-story ranch, a two-story colonial, or a craftsman with a full-width porch. It runs entirely in your browser.

03

Your photo needs four taps

Openings on an uploaded photo are traced by hand, corner by corner. The fit is perspective-correct once you've marked the four corners.

04

A designer still measures

Nothing here replaces the measure visit. It replaces the part where you try to imagine it from a brochure.
