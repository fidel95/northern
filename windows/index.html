Double-Hung

Two sashes that both slide vertically, so you can vent from the top and keep a lower sash closed over a radiator or a sill full of plants. Both sashes tilt in for cleaning from inside the room.

The most common replacement in central Indiana housing stock, and the style that reads correctly on almost every colonial, ranch, and four-square in the metro.

Casement

Hinged at the side and cranked outward, which means the sash seals against the frame under wind pressure instead of resting in a track. It is the tightest-sealing window we sell.

A crank is easier to reach across a countertop or a deep sink than a sash you have to lean over and lift.

Awning

Hinged at the top and opening outward from the bottom, so the sash becomes its own small roof. You can leave it cracked in a spring rain without water coming in.

Frequently paired underneath a picture window to add ventilation without breaking up the view.

Sliding

One or both sashes glide horizontally in a track. There are no cranks and no sashes to lift, which makes it the simplest window in the catalog to operate over a long service life.

Well suited to wide openings above a kitchen sink or in a walkout basement where the opening is wider than it is tall.

Bay & Bow

A bay projects from the wall in three angled planes; a bow curves through four or five. Either adds usable depth to the room — enough for a window seat, a reading chair, or a run of plants.

These are structural. We build the head and seat board, insulate the cavity, and flash the roof as part of the install; expect closer to ten weeks from order.

Common questions

Ratings, timelines,
and the warranty.

If the answer you need isn't here, a designer will walk through it during the consultation — or call (765) 376-1197.

What energy rating should I look for in Indiana?

Indiana sits in the ENERGY STAR Northern climate zone. Look for a U-factor of 0.27 or lower; our standard Low-E package rates 0.27 and the triple-pane package reaches 0.20.

How long does installation take?

Most whole-home window projects are installed in one to three days. A single room is typically finished in a single day, with furniture back in place before the crew leaves.

How long from order to install?

Because every unit is built to your measured opening, expect six to eight weeks from signed order to installation day. Bay and bow assemblies can run closer to ten weeks.

What does the warranty cover?

Lifetime coverage on glass and frame, including seal failure and obstructive glass, plus lifetime labor on our own installation. The warranty transfers once to a new owner.

Do you replace the full frame or just the sash?

We quote both. A pocket replacement keeps sound existing frames and costs less; a full-frame replacement is the right call when the sill or jamb has water damage, and we will tell you which we found during the measure visit.
